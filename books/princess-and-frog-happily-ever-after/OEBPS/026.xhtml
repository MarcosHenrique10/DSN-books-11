“There she is!” Ray cried, looking up at
the night sky. “The sweetest firefly in all
creation!”

Evangeline was really the Evening
Star! Louis began to play his trumpet,
and Naveen asked Tiana to dance.
As they twirled under Ray’s light,
Tiana realized that she had
learned something from the
prince, too. Maybe a little
bit of fun and romance
wasn’t so bad after all.
