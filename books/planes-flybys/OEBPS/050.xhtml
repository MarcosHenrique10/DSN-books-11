“YAAAAAAAAAH!” Dusty hollered. He managed to
 pull up just in time and blasted into the sky!

“WOO HOO HOOOOOO!” Dusty whooped. Now this was
 more like it—speeding along, just like a jet! But then his
 engine began to sputter. He had burned through all his fuel!

Luckily, just as his engine stopped completely, Dusty
 spotted the runway up ahead. He was going to make it!
