Tiana walked over to where her friend Charlotte was sitting.
 She told Charlotte what the man had said.

“Oh, Tia,” Charlotte cooed. “That’s Leon
 Robere! He told me he was one of the
 most famous chefs in the world!
 I saw him here last week,
 too. He just opened up a
 restaurant in town, and—
oh! You must try his
 gumbo. It’s divine.”

Tiana looked back at
 Leon. He was happily
 eating some beignets.
 When he saw Tiana, he
 frowned and put down
 the beignets.
