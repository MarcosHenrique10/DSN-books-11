“I have an idea,” she said.
 “I will teach you how to
 make a basic gumbo. Then
 you can add some twists to
 make it your own! In return,
 you will tell everyone that
 you borrowed my recipe.”

Leon smiled and shook
 Tiana’s hand. “You’ve got
 yourself a deal,” he replied.
 “But I have one more favor
 to ask. Could I have a bowl
 of your gumbo?”

Tiana laughed. “One
 bowl of Tiana’s Top-Secret
 Gumbo coming right up!”
