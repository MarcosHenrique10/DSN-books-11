But Leon did not apologize.

“Are you calling me a thief?” he yelled when Tiana confronted
 him the next day. “I would never use another chef’s recipe.
 Especially not a bad recipe!”

Tiana was shocked. What would she do now? Then she had

an idea. “Okay . . .” she
 began. “If you didn’t take
 my recipe, then prove it. I
 challenge you to a gumbo
 cook-off tomorrow at noon.
 No cookbooks or recipes.”

Leon paused for a
 moment. Then he puffed out
 his chest and said, “Fine! We
 will cook at my restaurant.”
