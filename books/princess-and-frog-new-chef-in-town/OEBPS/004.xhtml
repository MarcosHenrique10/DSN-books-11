Tiana dipped a spoon into a pot of bubbling stew. Mmmm, she
 thought as she took a taste. Just enough spice! She made a note in
 her secret recipe book.

She was working on a brand-new dish at her restaurant. The
 restaurant was always packed with people who came from near
 and far to try Tiana’s food—especially her gumbo. Tiana hung her
 apron on its hook. “Time to greet the customers!” she announced
 as she exited the kitchen.
