Fixed up, Dusty was more determined
than ever to win! He took off last but quickly
caught up to Ripslinger over the desert in
the final leg back to New York City.

Ripslinger wanted Dusty out of the race once and for all.
He closed in from above and forced Dusty toward the rocks!

Suddenly, Skipper came roaring out of the sky and began to
tangle with Ripslinger. Dusty couldn’t believe Skipper was flying!

Ripslinger tore up Skipper’s tail with his propeller before
zooming off. Dusty was worried about his coach, but Skipper
just laughed. “Go get him!” he ordered.
