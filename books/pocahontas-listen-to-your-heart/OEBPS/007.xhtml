Pocahontas hesitated. Leaves swirled around the pair as Smith
 moved closer and offered her his hand. “Who are you?” he asked.

“I’m Pocahontas,”
 she replied as she took
 his outstretched hand.
 When they touched,
 neither one wanted to
 let go. In her heart,
 Pocahontas knew that
 there was something
 special about this
 strange man.

Together, they
 laughed at Meeko,
 Pocahontas’s mischievous raccoon friend, who rummaged
 through Smith’s bag looking for food.
