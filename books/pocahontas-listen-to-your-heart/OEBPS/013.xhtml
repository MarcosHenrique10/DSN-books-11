Kocoum soon found Pocahontas and Smith. The warrior
 attacked Smith. All of a sudden, another settler showed up and
 killed Kocoum!

Furious, other warriors arrived and took John Smith
 prisoner. He was sentenced to die
 the next morning.

That night, Pocahontas visited
 John Smith. “I’m so sorry,” she
 told him, crying. “It would have
 been better if we’d never
 met. None of this would
 have happened.”

“Pocahontas, look at me,”
 he said tenderly. “I’d rather
 die tomorrow than live a hundred
 years without knowing you.”
