Later, when Pocahontas and her friend Nakoma were
 gathering corn, John Smith appeared.

“Please don’t say anything,” Pocahontas whispered to her
 friend before leaving with Smith.

Nakoma was too shocked to respond. She kept quiet but felt
 uncomfortable as she watched the two figures disappear into
 the evening shadows. She had been warned that the settlers were
 dangerous and was worried about her friend.
