Mother Gothel took Rapunzel back to the tower, but Rapunzel kept thinking about all she had seen. Suddenly, all the pieces fell into place. “I’m the lost princess!” she realized at last.

Instantly, Mother Gothel stopped pretending to be nice. She tied Rapunzel up, and then when Flynn came to try to save her, she attacked him.

Flynn was hurt, and Rapunzel knew she could heal him with her hair. “I’ll stay with you,” she promised Mother Gothel. “Just let me save him.”

“Swear it,” said Mother Gothel. And Rapunzel did.
