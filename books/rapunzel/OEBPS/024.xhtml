Flynn went ashore and tried to give the crown to the Stabbington brothers—but the thieves knocked him out. Then they found Rapunzel and told her Flynn had traded the crown to them for her.

“No!” Rapunzel cried. She tried to run away, but she couldn’t. Suddenly, Mother Gothel rushed and saved Rapunzel from the thieves! Rapunzel didn’t know that Mother Gothel had told the brothers to capture her.
