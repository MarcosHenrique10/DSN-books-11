“Wake, Pinocchio! Skip and run! Good Geppetto
 needs a son!” Pinocchio blinked his eyes and raised
 his wooden arms.

“I can move!” he cried. “I’m a real boy!”

“No,” the Blue Fairy said sadly. “You have life, but
 to become a real boy, you must prove yourself brave,
 truthful, and unselfish.”

And Jiminy Cricket would help.
