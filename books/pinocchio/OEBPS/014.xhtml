“You promised to go right home!” Jiminy cried.

“But Foulfellow says I need a rest after my
 terrible experience.”

They came to a coach bound for Pleasure
 Island. It was pulled by small donkeys and filled
 with noisy boys. As Pinocchio climbed aboard,
 Jiminy saw the evil-looking coachman slip
 Foulfellow a heavy bag. Again the fox had sold
 Pinocchio!
