After boarding a ferry, the coach docked at Pleasure Island. Here
 streets were paved with cookies, and fountains spouted lemonade.

Pinocchio soon made friends with a young bully called
 Lampwick who was always in the middle of mischief.

Jiminy was not happy. He shouted at the puppet to go home.

“Don’t tell me you’re scared of a beetle!” Lampwick snickered.

Jiminy was about to march off when all of a sudden Lampwick
 and Pinocchio groaned.
