Dear Pinocchio,

I heard you had gone to Pleasure Island, so Figaro,
 Cleo, and I started off in a small boat to find you. Just
 as we came in sight of the island, out of the sea rose
 Monstro, the giant whale. He opened his jaws; in we
 went. Now, dear son, we are living in the belly of
 the whale. But there is very little to eat here, and
 we cannot exist much longer, so I fear you will
 never again see

Your loving father,

Geppetto
