For a while, they were both silent, too sad to
 speak. Then Pinocchio stood tall and said, “I am
 going to save my father!”

Just then a dove wearing a golden crown
 appeared. “I will take you,” she said. “Climb on.”
 Then she spread her wings and flew and flew until
 they reached the seashore.

Pinocchio and Jiminy did not know that the dove
 was the Blue Fairy in disguise, and that it was she
 who had brought them Geppetto’s letter.
