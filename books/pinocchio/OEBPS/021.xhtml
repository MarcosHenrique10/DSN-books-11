When the dove was out of sight, Pinocchio tied a big
 stone to his donkey tail. Then he and Jiminy leaped off
 the cliff into the ocean below.

As soon as they reached the sandy bottom, Pinocchio
 scrambled to his feet. “Come on,” he said. “Let’s find
 Monstro.”

“We’ll never find him,” muttered Jiminy. “We’re
 probably looking in the wrong ocean!”
