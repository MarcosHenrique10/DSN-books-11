“Hello?” Pocahontas called. “I promise not to
 hurt you. I just want to know why you’re taking our
 corn.”

There was no answer. Flit flew inside to get
 a better look, but before Pocahontas knew it,
 Flit was zooming back past her. Something had
 thrown Flit out of the den, and now the poor
 hummingbird’s beak was stuck in a tree!

As Pocahontas pulled her friend free, she
 saw a raccoon run out of the den and
 into the woods.
