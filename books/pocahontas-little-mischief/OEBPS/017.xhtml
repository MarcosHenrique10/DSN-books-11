As the sun began to set,
 Pocahontas saw her father’s
 canoe pull up to the shore. She
 ran to greet him, with Meeko
 following close behind.

“How did you fare today,
 Daughter?” he asked. “I hope
 there wasn’t any trouble.”

“Nothing we couldn’t
 handle,” Pocahontas said,
 smiling. “Right, Meeko?”

“Ah, just a little mischief,
 then?” Pocahontas’s father
 returned her smile.

“Exactly.”
