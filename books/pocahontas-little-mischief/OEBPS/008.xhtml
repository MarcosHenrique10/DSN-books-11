Pocahontas looked around
 and saw a broken branch on
 the ground. “Something
 stepped on this branch to
 make it break. I think our
 visitor went this way.”

A little farther into
 the forest, Pocahontas
 noticed a set of claw
 prints in the soft earth.
 And then she saw a little
 bit of fur on a berry
 branch. “We must be
 getting close,” Pocahontas
 said. She soon tracked the
 visitor to a small den.
