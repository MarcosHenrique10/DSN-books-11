The rest of the morning,
 Pocahontas was busy in the
 village. She repaired wigwams,
 gathered herbs for Kekata the
 medicine man, and taught the
 young boys and girls how to
 track.

By that afternoon, the only
 thing Pocahontas had left to do
 was check on the fields. But on
 her way, she was stopped
by Alawa, one of the youngest
 girls in the tribe. “Pocahontas!
 A crow took my favorite
 necklace,” Alawa said sadly.
 “Could you help me get it back?”
