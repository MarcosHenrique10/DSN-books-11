“You did it, Meeko!”
 Pocahontas said. The new
 friends went into the village
 to find Alawa. When she saw
 Pocahontas, the girl ran over,
 excited.

“Did you get my necklace,
 Pocahontas?” she asked.

“I had some help from my
 friend here,” Pocahontas
 said. Shyly, Meeko held out
 Alawa’s necklace.

“Oh, thank you!” she
 cried. She grabbed Meeko
 and gave him a big hug.
