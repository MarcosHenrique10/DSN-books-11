The light was fading, and Pocahontas knew it was time to
 say good-bye to Meeko. “Stay
 safe,” Pocahontas said as they
 walked toward the woods. “And
 stay out of the corn, too!”

Meeko started to climb a tree,
 but then he paused and turned
 back to Pocahontas.

“Yes?” Pocahontas asked with

a smile. Meeko ran back to Pocahontas and leaped into her arms.
 Pocahontas laughed. “Don’t worry, we’ll see each other again. I can
 visit you in the woods anytime.”

Meeko looked up at her happily. “And I can bring you any extra
 corn when I do,” she added.

Meeko liked the sound of that!
