Pocahontas looked up to where the young girl
 was pointing. The crow had hidden her necklace
 high in a tree! It was too dangerous for Flit to fly
 into a crow’s nest. And the tree had no branches
 for Pocahontas to climb.

Before Pocahontas could think
 of a plan, a woman ran up to her.
 “Pocahontas, an animal has broken into
 the corn! Look, this cob is half-eaten!”

Pocahontas looked
 at Alawa. “I’m sorry,
 but we have to figure
 out who’s stealing our
 food. I’ll help you get
 the necklace soon, I
 promise.”
