It’s a windy Halloween morning, and Bob is decorating the house.
 Rolly is already in his costume, but he can’t find his brother.

“Bingo? Where are you?” he calls out.

“HAPPY HOWL-OWEEN!” Bingo shouts, jumping from

behind a pile of pumpkins.

“You spooked me!” Rolly giggles.

“I’m a pup-kin. Get it? A puppy and a pumpkin,” Rolly says proudly.
