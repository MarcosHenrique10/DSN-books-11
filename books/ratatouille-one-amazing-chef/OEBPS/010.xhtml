Skinner wanted Linguini to cook again, but Linguini didn’t
 know the first thing about cooking. Linguini realized that
 Remy could help, and they came up with a plan: Remy
 would sit in Linguini’s chef’s hat and steer him by the hair.

They practiced at Linguini’s home with Linguini
 wearing a blindfold. Before long, Linguini was
 chopping, mixing, and cooking, with Remy directing.
