He chased Remy out and to the river. The selfish chef couldn’t let the
 rat escape!

Remy jumped from the riverbank onto a boat, using the papers as
 wings. Skinner jumped after him . . . but didn’t make it! Splash! The
 angry man fell into the water and watched Remy get away.

By the time Skinner returned to the restaurant, Remy had told
 Linguini the truth. As the new owner of Gusteau’s, Linguini fired his
 old boss.
