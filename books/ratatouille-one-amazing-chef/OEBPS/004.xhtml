Deep in the French countryside lived a rat named Remy.
 Remy dreamed of being a great chef. He often sneaked into
 the living room of the old woman who owned the house to
 watch his idol, Chef Auguste Gusteau, on TV.

One day, Remy learned that Gusteau had died of a broken
 heart when his restaurant lost its five-star rating. Remy was
 so shocked, he didn’t hear the old woman wake up.

The old woman chased Remy and his family out of the
 house. They escaped into a drain, and somehow Remy ended
 up in Gusteau’s restaurant in Paris! It was Remy’s dream
 come true!
