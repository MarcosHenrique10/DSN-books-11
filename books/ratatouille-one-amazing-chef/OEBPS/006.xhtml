In the kitchen, a young man
 named Linguini was giving the chef,
 Skinner, a letter.

Linguini explained that his mother
 had known Gusteau. “I think she
 hoped I could get a job here,” he said.

Skinner hired Linguini as the
 garbage boy, and it did not take long
 for things to go wrong. He knocked
 over a pot of soup, and tried to fix it
 by adding water and spices.
