Remy knew the soup would taste
 awful. He tried to make it better by
 adding ingredients to the pot.

Linguini saw Remy, and hid him just
 as Skinner came in for the soup.

Skinner saw Linguini holding the
 soup ladle. “How dare you cook in my
 kitchen?” he yelled. But then the soup
 was served to a food critic who loved it!
