The next day, Anton Ego, the critic who had taken away
 Gusteau’s five-star rating, came to review Linguini’s cooking.
 Remy was there to help Linguini. But then somebody saw him.

“Rat!” the cooks cried out.

“Don’t touch him!” said Linguini. “The truth is, I have no
 talent at all. But this rat—he’s the cook.”

All the cooks left, including Colette. They were disgusted.
 Linguini didn’t know what to do. He and Remy needed help.
